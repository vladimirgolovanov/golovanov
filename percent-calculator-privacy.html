Privacy Policy

Percent Calculator (me.golovanov.percentcalc) · Effective: 7 Sep 2026

Short version: Percent Calculator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information we collect

None that leaves your device. The app does not create accounts, does not use analytics, contains no ads, and includes no third‑party tracking. We do not have a server and receive no data about you.

Data stored on your device

To make the app work, a small amount of information is saved locally in the app’s private storage and never sent to us:

  • A count of how many free calculations you’ve made today and the current date, so the app can enforce the daily free limit.
  • Whether you own the one‑time “unlock unlimited” purchase, so the limit stays removed.

This data is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Clipboard

When you tap a result to copy it, the app writes that number to your device clipboard. The app does not read your clipboard or send its contents anywhere.

Purchases

The optional one‑time unlock is sold through Google Play’s billing system. Google Play processes the payment and holds your purchase record; the app never sees or stores your payment details and only receives a signal that the unlock is owned. Google’s handling of that transaction is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app requests only the billing permission required by Google Play’s in‑app purchase library. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or other sensitive data.

Children

The app is a general‑purpose calculator, is not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
